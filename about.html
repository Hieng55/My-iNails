Welcome to My iNails

Beauty with comfort at its heart.

Located at 8110 W Union Hills Dr Ste 300, Glendale, AZ 85308, My iNails is your local non-toxic nail salon for beautiful nails, relaxing care, and exceptional service. From the moment you step inside, our friendly team strives to create an atmosphere where you can unwind, feel refreshed, and leave with confidence.

We offer a full range of professional nail and spa services, including manicures, spa pedicures, nail enhancements, gel nails, dip powder, custom nail art, waxing, and more. Every service is performed with high-quality products, strict cleanliness standards, and close attention to detail.

Your comfort and satisfaction are our top priorities. Whether you are preparing for a special event or simply treating yourself to well-deserved self-care, we are committed to an experience that helps you look and feel your best.

Welcome to My iNails!

Located at 8110 W Union Hills Dr Ste 300, Glendale, AZ 85308, My iNails is your local non-toxic nail salon for beautiful nails, relaxing care, and exceptional service. From the moment you step inside, our friendly team strives to create an atmosphere where you can unwind, feel refreshed, and leave with confidence.

We offer a full range of professional nail and spa services, including manicures, spa pedicures, acrylics, gel nails, dip powder, custom nail art, waxing, and more. Every service is performed with high-quality products, strict cleanliness standards, and attention to detail — ensuring results that are not only beautiful but long-lasting.

At My iNails, your comfort and satisfaction are our top priorities. Whether you’re preparing for a special event or simply treating yourself to some well-deserved self-care, we are committed to delivering a relaxing experience that helps you look and feel your best.

Visit us at 8110 W Union Hills Dr Ste 300, Glendale, AZ 85308 or call us at 602-481-6121 to book your appointment today — relax, refresh, and shine with My iNails.

Welcome to Star Nails & Spa!

Located at 1416 Military Rd, Benton, AR 72015, Star Nails & Spa is your local destination for beautiful nails, relaxing spa experiences, and exceptional customer care. From the moment you step inside, our friendly and talented team strives to create an atmosphere where you can unwind, feel refreshed, and leave with confidence.

We offer a full range of professional nail and spa services, including manicures, spa pedicures, acrylics, gel nails, dip powder, custom nail art, waxing, and more. Every service is performed with high-quality products, strict cleanliness standards, and attention to detail — ensuring results that are not only beautiful but long-lasting.

At Star Nails & Spa, your comfort and satisfaction are our top priorities. Whether you’re preparing for a special event or simply treating yourself to some well-deserved self-care, we are committed to delivering a relaxing experience that helps you look and feel your best.

Visit us at 1416 Military Rd, Benton, AR 72015 or call us at 501-315-2344 to book your appointment today — relax, refresh, and shine with Star Nails & Spa.
